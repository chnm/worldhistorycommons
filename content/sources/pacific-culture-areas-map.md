This map illustrates the three dominant cultures in Oceania, Micronesia, Melanesia, and Polynesia, and which islands occupy each region. This map successfully highlights the number of island nations/cultures and the overall size of Oceania. This easy-to-read map would be useful for students to study an oft-forgotten region. Combined with other resources that discuss the arrival of people to different islands, this map could be useful to discuss canoe migration and cultural dispersal in the region.
